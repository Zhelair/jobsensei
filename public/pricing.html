Free

EUR 0. 465 hosted AI credits per allowance period, equivalent to 15 requests at 31 credits per request. Use the application tracker and local workspace, and try hosted preparation tools within the allowance.

Pro — EUR 10 per month plus applicable tax

Planned standard recurring offer: 25,110 hosted credits per paid allowance period, equivalent to 810 requests at 31 credits each. An interview turn or separate AI action counts as a request; this is not 810 complete interviews. Paid periods and included credits are shown in Account. Unused allowance does not roll over.

When live checkout is enabled, it shows the total including applicable tax, monthly renewal and seller terms before purchase. New subscriptions must match this EUR 10 monthly catalog price. Sandbox checkout is for testing and is not a live purchase.

BYOK

Use a compatible AI provider key with a free JobSensei account; Pro is optional for BYOK. Your provider charges separately. Vision review requires a compatible vision-capable model. Browser voice availability varies by device.

Renewal and cancellation

A monthly subscription renews until canceled. Cancel before the next billing date using Account or the receipt/provider portal. Cancellation normally preserves access through the paid period. See the Refund Policy for withdrawal and refund requests; cancellation alone does not request a refund.

What is included

Application tracking, company-preparation notes, CV/JD analysis, cover letters, story preparation, interview practice and interactive learning. Hosted actions consume the account allowance. Local workspace data stays in the current browser unless you export/import a backup.
