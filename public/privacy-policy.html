Your local workspace

Your resume, projects, applications, stories, learning history and notes are stored in this browser. They are not automatically synchronized by signing in. Export/import transfers a backup you choose. Local storage is not a promise that text you submit to AI stays on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Authentication sends your email and sign-in information to Supabase. Google sign-in uses openid, email and profile information; it does not request Gmail, Drive or Calendar access.
  • Hosted AI sends the prompt and selected context, such as CV text, job descriptions, notes and conversation turns, through the JobSensei server to DeepSeek. BYOK sends requests to your configured AI provider. Only submit material you want processed for that action.
  • Company research uses the selected AI provider, without a separate live search service.
  • Feedback sends your message, optional name and authenticated email through Resend to the developer. It does not automatically attach your resume, transcript or diagnostic files.
  • Voice recognition and playback use browser/device speech services, which may process audio remotely. JobSensei does not operate an audio-recording store. Recognized text you submit becomes AI conversation input.

Account, billing and security records

Supabase stores account identifiers/email, approved-device records, credit balances and periods, plan/grant records, and billing identifiers and events needed to operate access. Paddle processes payment details when used for checkout; JobSensei does not receive your full card details. Existing Buy Me a Coffee transactions remain with that provider. Resend supports authentication email and feedback delivery. Vercel hosts the app and server functions.

Extension capture

The JobSensei Capture extension uses job-page information only to let you review and send company, role, job URL and selected or visible job-description text to your JobSensei workspace. It does not sell that data or use it for advertising. Use of extension data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics measures visits, referrers, approximate countries, operating systems, browsers and product events. Vercel describes this analytics service as cookie-free and anonymized. Speed Insights measures real-user performance. These services do not need your resume or AI conversation content. Necessary sign-in/device storage is described in the Storage Notice.

Purposes and legal bases

Authentication, requested AI features, access management and billing support performance of the service contract. Security and abuse prevention support legitimate interests in protecting users and the service. Legal obligations may require retaining billing records. Optional requests are handled for their stated purpose. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Local workspace data remains until you remove it from this browser. Account deletion and local clearing are separate operations. Hosted account/access records are used while the account is active and handled by the account-deletion process, subject to records needed for legal obligations or disputes. Security request metadata includes request type, status, timestamps and hashed IP/content metadata, not feedback message text; remaining service-request metadata is purged on a later request after 32 days. Feedback emails remain until resolved and removed; contact us to request deletion. AI, email, hosting and payment providers also apply their own retention rules.

Providers and international processing

Providers may process information outside the EEA. Contact us for the processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards for your use of the service. Provider notices: Paddle, Supabase, Vercel, Resend and your selected AI provider.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact privacy@jobsensei.app. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection. Export your local workspace before clearing data or requesting deletion.
