This is the technical storage companion to the Privacy Policy.

Workspace and preferences

Browser local storage keeps your projects, resume text, applications, learning history, notes, stories and preferences. A configured BYOK key is stored on this device for provider requests. Do not use a shared browser profile for private preparation.

Necessary account storage

Authentication sessions, access tokens and device identifiers support sign-in and approved-device controls. These are separate from your local workspace and from payment-provider storage.

Analytics and external services

Vercel describes Web Analytics as cookie-free. Speed Insights measures performance. Sign-in and payment providers may use their own necessary storage when you interact with them; consult their notices linked from the Privacy Policy.

Your controls

Export a backup in Settings before clearing data. Clearing browser storage can remove your local workspace and sign you out. It does not delete your hosted account, replenish credits or cancel a subscription. Use Account for those separate actions.
