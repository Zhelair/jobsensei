Pricing Terms of Service Privacy Policy

JobSensei is a digital preparation service. New Pro subscriptions are sold through Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle handles payments and refunds under its Buyer Terms and Refund Policy. Mandatory consumer rights always apply.

Consumer withdrawal and faulty services

Consumers in the EU/EEA generally have 14 days to withdraw from an online service contract. Different rules can apply to digital content and services. Immediate access or AI usage does not by itself remove a statutory withdrawal right. Any exception or lawful proportionate charge depends on applicable law, the contract classification, and the required information and express consent. Rights concerning defective or misdescribed services remain available.

Renewals

Cancel before the next billing date to prevent renewal. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the paid period. Renewal refunds are reviewed under Paddle policy and applicable law; a fresh statutory withdrawal period does not necessarily arise on every monthly renewal. Contact Paddle promptly if a renewal was unexpected.

Discretionary refunds and abuse prevention

  • Outside mandatory rights, refunds are discretionary and considered case by case under Paddle policy.
  • Usage, request timing, technical problems and evidence of repeated refund abuse may inform a discretionary decision.
  • Usage limits and anti-abuse measures do not override mandatory withdrawal or refund rights.
  • A full refund for a paid period ends the corresponding paid access. Partial refunds are reviewed separately. A refund and cancellation are separate actions; confirm future billing has also stopped if you want to leave.

How to cancel

Use Manage subscription in JobSensei Account, the Paddle Customer Portal link in your receipt, or Paddle buyer support. Clearing local workspace data does not cancel billing. Account deletion attempts to cancel linked Paddle subscriptions, but you should retain the cancellation confirmation. Deletion does not automatically request a refund.

How to request a refund

Visit Paddle buyer support or use your receipt support link. Eligible EU transactions also have a withdrawal option in the Paddle Customer Portal. You can contact JobSensei through Account → Feedback & support for product issues. Include your purchase email, transaction reference and charge date. A reason helps with discretionary requests but is not required to exercise an applicable statutory withdrawal right. Never send card numbers or payment credentials.

Chargebacks and duplicate charges

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase or believe a payment was made in error, contact Paddle so the issue can be reviewed and resolved. This does not restrict your lawful right to dispute a charge.

Legacy purchases and voluntary support

Earlier Buy Me a Coffee purchases remain with that provider. Use the original receipt/provider account for cancellation or purchase support, or contact JobSensei through Account feedback. New Paddle terms do not retroactively replace the terms of an earlier purchase. Voluntary support does not create a new Paddle subscription.
