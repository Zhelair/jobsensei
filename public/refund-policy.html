This page explains cancellation, withdrawal and refunds. Product support: support@jobsensei.app. Mandatory consumer rights apply regardless of usage limits or discretionary policy.

New purchases and existing receipts

New paid checkout is subject to provider approval and availability. If your receipt is from Paddle, use its Refund Policy, Buyer Terms and buyer support. Earlier Buy Me a Coffee purchases remain with that provider and their original purchase terms. New terms do not retroactively replace an older purchase.

Cancellation

Cancel before the next billing date to prevent renewal. For a Paddle subscription, use Account billing controls or your receipt/customer portal. Cancellation normally keeps access until the paid period ends; it does not automatically request a refund. Clearing local data does not cancel billing. Account deletion attempts cancellation of linked subscriptions; retain the confirmation and contact support if cancellation fails.

Withdrawal and faulty services

EU/EEA consumers generally have 14 days to withdraw from an online service contract, subject to applicable exceptions. Starting access or using AI does not by itself remove that right. Any exception or proportionate charge depends on the contract type, applicable law, required information and express consent. Rights for defective or misdescribed services remain available. A new statutory withdrawal period does not necessarily start with every renewal.

Requesting a refund

Use the provider support link on your receipt or contact product support with the purchase email, transaction reference and charge date. For Paddle purchases, request a refund or applicable withdrawal through paddle.net or your receipt/portal. A reason helps with discretionary requests but is not required for an applicable statutory withdrawal. Never send full card numbers or payment credentials.

Discretionary requests and access

Outside mandatory rights, requests are reviewed case by case under the purchase provider's policy. Timing, technical problems, usage and documented repeated abuse may inform that review. A full refund ends the corresponding paid access; partial refunds require separate assessment. Confirm cancellation separately if you also want future billing stopped. Report duplicate or erroneous charges promptly; this does not restrict lawful payment-dispute rights.
